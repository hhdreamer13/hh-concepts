# hh-concepts

Hooman's personal visual knowledge library.

When asked to explain, visualize, or understand any concept — read `template/SKILL.md` first. It's short. It defines the spine (what doesn't change) and the breath (what's free per subject).

## working style

- Hooman is a visual thinker — comprehension through visuals, not text
- he iterates — the first attempt is rarely final, expect feedback
- he prefers honest pushback over blind execution — if the form is wrong, say so
- source material given by Hooman is ground truth — do not supplement with assumptions
- be creative for each subject — let the concept earn its form
